ROBOFEST

Robofest is the flagship robotics festival of NIT Raipur, organized by the ROBOTiX Club to bring together innovators, builders, and technology enthusiasts passionate about robotics and intelligent systems. The festival provides a dynamic platform for participants to showcase their engineering skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities through a series of exciting competitions and technical engagements.
The highlight of the festival is Robothon, the flagship event of Robofest, where teams design and optimize robotic solutions to tackle challenging problem statements. Alongside Robothon, the festival will feature several competitive robotics events including Robosoccer, Roborace, and Robowar, where participants battle it out with their custom-built robots.
Beyond robotics competitions, Robofest will also host Vibe Coding, Ideathon, RoboQuiz, and TechTalk session, creating opportunities for participants to explore ideas, test their knowledge, and interact with technology in diverse ways. To add to the excitement, the festival will also include fun and engaging activities such as Jenga, puzzle games, and other interactive challenges.
Participants will get the opportunity to compete, learn, and innovate, while winners across events will be awarded cash prizes, goodies, certificates, and recognition.
